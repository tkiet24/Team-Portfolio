Who we are

We Are OurPortfolio.

OurPortfolio is a software outsourcing team of three university students who share one belief: great software is the result of great collaboration between design, engineering, and architecture.

We are a student team from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, studying Software Engineering. What started as a class project became a serious team with a shared vision: to build real products that solve real problems, applying industry-grade practices from day one.

01
User First, Always
Every decision starts with the user. We design and build with empathy, not assumptions.
02
Quality Over Speed
We write clean code, document our decisions, and test before we ship.
03
Built to Scale
From architecture to deployment, we think about what happens when the product grows.
